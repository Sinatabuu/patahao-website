Delete your Pata Hao account

You can request deletion of your Pata Hao account and associated personal data without needing to access the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@patahao.app with the subject Pata Hao Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address or phone number associated with your Pata Hao account. Do not send your password or payment PIN.
  3. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

What is deleted or retained?

After verification, we will process deletion of account profile information and associated personal data where deletion is appropriate. Certain transaction, payment, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legally required records may need to be retained for a limited period under applicable obligations.

Before accepting requests: Pata Hao must activate the support email, confirm the operational deletion procedure and applicable retention periods, and ensure requests are actually fulfilled.
