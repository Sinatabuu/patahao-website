Become a Partner

Your first step starts here.

No cash transactions. No exceptions.

Pata Hao is a no-cash platform. All cash transactions with customers or property owners are prohibited. Partners must not request, collect, accept or make cash payments. All payments must go through approved Pata Hao channels.

Any violation results in immediate termination of the partnership.

We welcome people with local property knowledge, property owners, agencies and people ready to learn.

  • Accurate listings and owner permission
  • Respectful customer service
  • Mandatory office training and assessment
  • Payments handled through approved Pata Hao channels

Keep identity documents for your office visit. Do not submit ID numbers, scans, home addresses or references here. Sensitive verification records will be kept in controlled physical office files.
