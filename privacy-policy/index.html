Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy describes how Pata Hao handles information when you use its mobile application and related services. Pata Hao provides property discovery, viewing coordination, and related customer and partner workflows in Kenya.

Information you provide

Depending on how you use the service, information may include your name, contact details, account credentials, property listing details, photographs or videos you choose to upload, viewing preferences, messages, and information supplied during property or partner verification.

Information generated by using the service

We may process account activity, viewing requests and their statuses, payment and refund records, credits, notifications, and technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. If location features are used, location information may be processed to support those features. Camera, photo, or file access is used when you choose to upload relevant content.

How information is used

We use information to manage accounts, display listings, coordinate viewings, support customers and property partners, maintain transaction records, prevent misuse, and improve service reliability.

Sharing

Information relevant to a property or viewing may be shared with the customer or property partner involved in that workflow. Service providers supporting hosting, communications, and payments may process information as needed to deliver their services. We do not describe any payment method as live unless it is enabled in the app.

Payments

The app may record viewing fees, payment references, payment status, credits, and refund requests. Payment availability and methods depend on the app's current configuration. This website does not collect payment details.

Security and retention

We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards to protect information. Records may be retained where needed for transactions, disputes, security, and applicable legal obligations. Deletion requests may not remove records that must lawfully be retained.

Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information by contacting us. See our Account Deletion page for instructions.

Children

Pata Hao is intended for adults aged 18 and older.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and publish the revised effective date here. For privacy inquiries, contact support@patahao.app.

Publication review: Before submitting this policy to Google Play, Pata Hao must verify these descriptions against the released app, its actual service providers, data retention practices, and functioning support contact.
